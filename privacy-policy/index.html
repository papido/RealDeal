Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RecipeFlow collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data when you use the app.

Data We Collect

  • Account data: email address, username, and user ID.
  • Profile data: optional address, phone number, profile image, currency preference, AI credits, and last sign-in timestamp.
  • Recipe and planner data: saved recipes (parsed ingredients), planner entries, planned dates/times, and recipe website links.
  • Cart data: ingredients you add, edit, or delete in your cart.
  • Advertising/diagnostic data: app may use Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may process device identifiers and ad diagnostics.

How We Use Data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To save recipes, planner cards, and cart content.
  • To support rewarded ads and improve app reliability.

Data Storage and Providers

  • Firebase Authentication is used for sign-in and account identity.
  • Cloud Firestore is used to store user profile and app content under your user account.
  • Google Sign-In may be used if you choose Google login.
  • Google Mobile Ads may process data for ad serving and measurement.

Provider policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared with service providers only as needed to operate app functionality described above.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can edit profile information in the app.
  • You can request account deletion from the Profile tab. Deletion removes your account and associated user data stored by the app.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact the app publisher at support contact details listed in the store page.
